Events & catering

Event bookings carry the catering selections, estimated food total and no-outside-catering agreement in the booking record. Staff can confirm the booking after availability and payment review.

Venue space is free for applicable event/group bookings; customers pay for Kiteezi food and drinks. Outside catering is not permitted.
