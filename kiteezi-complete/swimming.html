School swimming

A reliable place for school groups.

School swimming is one of Kiteezi's regular weekday activities. Groups can reserve sessions in advance so the reception and coaching team can prepare the pool schedule.

  • Monday–Friday during school hours
  • Typical session length: one hour
  • Group booking and attendance tracking
  • Coach-supported swimming sessions
  • Reception coordination for arrivals and payments

Training

Swimming coaching & skills development.

Structured coaching can be arranged for customers who want to learn, improve technique or build confidence in the water. A standard program is designed around a two-week course, with final pricing and session times managed by the center.
