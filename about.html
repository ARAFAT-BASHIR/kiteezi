About Kiteezi

A place built around people.

Kiteezi Recreational Center brings hospitality, recreation, swimming, dining and outdoor space together in Kiteezi, Lusanjja, Kampala.

Our purpose

Make it easy to spend a good day together.

The center is designed for everyday visits as well as organized groups. Guests can swim, eat, play sports, relax in the gardens or plan a catered gathering.

Behind the scenes, Kiteezi is developing a central management platform so customer service, bookings, swimming schedules, restaurant operations, inventory, staff permissions and reporting can work from one system.

Our team

Clear responsibilities, one guest experience.

Management

The owner/Managing Director provides overall direction, supported by a General Manager for operational oversight.

Guest Services

The Receptionist also serves as on-site Manager, coordinating bookings, arrivals, customer enquiries and daily service.

Specialist Teams

Swimming coaches, Chef, Barista and grounds/cleaning staff each have focused responsibilities.
