Who we are

More than a place to visit.

Kiteezi Recreational Center is designed as a welcoming destination where people can spend time together, stay active, enjoy food and create memorable experiences.

Our activities include swimming, sports, restaurant service, outdoor recreation and event hosting. The center serves individuals, families, schools, teams, companies and private groups.

We aim to make visits straightforward: choose the activity you want, tell us what you need and let the team help coordinate the details.
