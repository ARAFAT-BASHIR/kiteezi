How it works

Simple event planning.

  • The Kiteezi garden venue is free.
  • Customers bring their own tents, tables and chairs.
  • Kiteezi provides agreed food and drinks.
  • Reception coordinates the booking and event requirements.
  • Final catering quantities and menu choices are confirmed before the event.
